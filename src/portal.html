right now

At Composite I work on models that predict your next move in the browser, with no prompt. I fine-tune them, use RL so they learn when not to act, and work on making them fast enough to feel instant. I also build the evals that score each part of a prediction on its own, and the datasets made from real browsing sessions.

the workshop

Things I've built.

things I did at caltech

  • A robot that plays mini-golf. It takes turns with a human player: it spots its club with a camera, picks it up, putts toward the hole, and nudges the ball off the walls. A five-joint SCARA arm over a C-shaped 4 ft × 3 ft course, built with Iñigo Jensen and Kimberly Miranda Reyes for ME/CS/EE 134 (2025). (code, report)
  • A robot that plays tennis. A simulated six-joint arm that tracks an incoming ball and hits it to a random target, with smooth, human-like swings. Built with Jihyun Jeon for ME 133a (2023), on top of the course's kinematics projects: Jacobians and trajectories in ROS 2. (code, report, kinematics projects)
  • Pandemaniac. Strategies for a competitive network game where teams pick seed nodes to spread across a graph, including one built on Thompson sampling. A team project for a Caltech networks course (2024). (code)
  • Connecting images to an LLM. A proposal to feed CLIP image embeddings into an LLM through a small mapping network, trained with self-critical sequence training, for EE 148 (2024). (proposal)
  • Diffusion models from the ground up. DDPM derivations, score matching, and training a diffusion model, for EE 148b (2024). (write-up, 14 MB)

other things

  • TomorrowCard. Student credit underwriting with agents: it verifies transcripts and résumés and estimates future earnings. 1st place at AGI House, built with Krish Mehta. (code, announcement)
  • Linklet. A personal AI advocate. It reads your GitHub, résumé and site, then answers questions about you in chat.
  • AEGIS. A walk-home safety companion on Gemini Live. It watches and listens, flags threats, tracks your route and can call a friend. Built with Krish Mehta.
  • GroceryAI. A kitchen helper that keeps track of what's in the pantry: tell it what you bought or used, by voice or text, and it updates the list, converting units along the way. Built with Krish Mehta.
  • Xactly. A shopping assistant that finds products from a conversation. (code)
  • Pengu Run. A multiplayer penguin platformer that runs in the browser. (code)
  • LLM watermarking. Spread-spectrum watermarks: hiding a statistical signal in LLM-generated text that can be detected later.
